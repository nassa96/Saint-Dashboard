# ☁️ Deploy SAINT CORE to Render

> **Deployment status: not verified from this workspace.** The blueprint
> (`render.yaml`) targets `main` and enables auto-deploy after the release is
> merged. The preview shown here is the development sandbox; Render service
> creation, environment secrets, and health still need to be verified in the
> Render account.

Render is a good fit: it runs your Node server 24/7, gives you an HTTPS URL, and
reads `render.yaml` automatically.

---

## Before you start
- A **Render account** (render.com) — free to create.
- This repo on **GitHub** (it already is: `nassa96/Saint-Dashboard`).
- No exchange API key is needed for the supported PAPER tester rollout. Do not configure trade-enabled keys for this release.

---

## Step 1 — Create the service from the blueprint
1. In Render: **New +** → **Blueprint**.
2. Connect your GitHub and pick **`nassa96/Saint-Dashboard`**.
3. Render detects `render.yaml` and shows a service called **saint-core**.
4. Click **Apply**. Render runs the lockfile-based build (`npm ci`) and starts the service with `npm start`.
5. `DASHBOARD_PASSWORD` and `SESSION_SECRET` are generated by Render. Retrieve the dashboard password from the service's Environment settings and share it with trusted testers through a separate secure channel.

> ⚠ **Plan note:** the blueprint uses the **Starter** plan so the bot runs 24/7.
> The **Free** plan *sleeps after ~15 min idle* — a sleeping bot places no trades
> and misses signals. If you deploy on Free to test, expect that. The persistent
> **disk** (for `data/`) also requires a paid instance; on Free, remove the `disk:`
> block from `render.yaml` (your portfolio/keys then reset on each redeploy).

---

## Step 2 — Verify generated secrets and safe mode
In the service's **Environment** tab, confirm Render has generated both secrets:

| Variable | What to verify |
|---|---|
| `DASHBOARD_PASSWORD` | **Required.** Render-generated and at least 16 characters; retrieve it there and share out-of-band with trusted testers. |
| `SESSION_SECRET` | Render-generated, stable, and at least 32 characters; keep it private and persistent. |

Do not add exchange credentials for a tester rollout. `TRADING_MODE=PAPER`,
`LIVE_TRADING_ENABLED=false`, and `LIVE_TRADING_CONFIRM` blank are intentional.

---

## Step 3 — Open your live dashboard
Render gives you a URL like `https://saint-core.onrender.com`.

1. Open it → log in with `DASHBOARD_PASSWORD`.
2. Confirm the market badge shows **LIVE data** (Render has internet, so real
   prices flow — unlike the dev sandbox).
3. Leave exchange credentials unconfigured for the tester rollout. If a balance display is specifically needed, use a separate read-only key and verify the scope at the venue; never use a withdrawal-enabled or trade-enabled key.

---

## Step 4 — Private PAPER tester rollout
1. Keep `TRADING_MODE=PAPER`, `LIVE_TRADING_ENABLED=false`, and on-chain writes off.
2. Use the dashboard's **Live funds readiness audit** to monitor blockers. Live
   arming is intentionally refused by this release; do not bypass it.
3. Monitor PAPER fills, logs, stale/mixed price warnings, and analytics for
   multiple weeks. `DATA: LIVE` means market quotes are live; it does not mean
   orders or profits are live.
4. This is one shared service: testers share the paper portfolio, exchange
   settings, operations controls, and logs. Restrict access to trusted testers.

## How the pieces map to Render
| Concern | Handled by |
|---|---|
| Port | Server honors `process.env.PORT`, binds `0.0.0.0` ✅ |
| Health check | `GET /api/health` (set in `render.yaml`) |
| Persistence | Disk mounted at `.../project/src/data` keeps PAPER portfolio/history; live arms are never restored after restart |
| Secrets | Dashboard password and session secret are generated by Render; never commit secrets |
| Auto-deploy | On for `main`; merged commits redeploy when this blueprint is connected to the Render service |

## Troubleshooting
- **Build fails:** check the Node version (blueprint pins `NODE_VERSION=20`).
- **App sleeps / stops trading:** you're on the Free plan — upgrade to Starter.
- **Dashboard loads but "SIM" data:** the public market-data host is unreachable from the Render region or outbound access failed. No exchange key is needed for public market prices.
- **Lost portfolio after redeploy:** you're missing the persistent disk (Free plan).
- **Can't arm live:** this is expected in the current release. Read
  `LIVE_READINESS.md`; do not add funds or bypass the readiness gate.

See **`LAUNCH.md`** for the PAPER rollout checklist, **`LIVE_READINESS.md`**
for the live-funds audit, and **`VENUES.md`** for connector details.
